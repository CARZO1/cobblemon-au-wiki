# 🎒 Your first steps

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Every visit starts in the <strong>Hub lobby</strong>. From there you can grab a starter, pick up a free kit, and head to Survival to build your first base.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🐾 1. Get started

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Choose your starter

Press **M** when you first join to open the starter screen and pick your partner.

If you already caught or were given a Pokémon before choosing, put it in a PC so your party is empty, then press **M** again.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Claim the Starter kit

Type `/kits` on the Hub and choose **Starter**. You'll get:

* a stone axe, pickaxe and shovel
* a **golden hoe** (this is your land claim tool)
* 32 torches
* 20 Poké Balls

You can claim it again every 30 days.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Head to Survival

Type `/survival` to go to the main world. Use `/rtp` to be dropped somewhere random in the wild, then find a spot you like.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Claim your land and set a home

Hold the golden hoe and mark two opposite corners of your base to claim it. Then stand inside and type `/home set <name>` so you can always get back with `/home tp <name>`.

There's more detail in the [FAQ](faq.md) under "Claiming land and homes".
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
✅ Once your base is claimed and your home is set, you're ready to explore.
{% endhint %}

***

## 🏙️ 2. Find your way around the Hub

Type `/warps` for a menu of every location, or use the shortcuts below.

| 📍 Location | 🔎 What it's for | ⌨️ Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🏥 **Pokémon Center** | Heal your team | `/pokecenter` |
| 🛒 **PokéMart** | Buy and sell supplies. Mega Rings, Z-Rings, Dynamax Bands and Tera Orbs are in the Cobblemon Supplies store at the back | `/pokemart` |
| 📜 **Quests** | Daily and weekly quests for money and crate keys | `/quest_hub` |
| 🎁 **Crates** | Open the crate keys you earn | `/crates` |
| 🎰 **Gacha Machines** | Spend Relic Coins on capsules | `/gacha` |
| 💪 **EV Training Tower** | Train your Pokémon's EVs | `/ev_tower` |
| 🏆 **Hall of Fame** | See who holds each region's Champion title | `/hall_of_fame` |
| 🌿 **Safari Zone** | Catch Pokémon with Safari Balls | `/safari` |
| 🏪 **Player Market** | Rent a plot and run your own shop | `/market` |
| ⚔️ **Gyms** | Challenge the gyms of Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and Sinnoh | `/gyms` |

You can get back to the lobby at any time with `/hub`.

***

## ❤️ 3. Keep your team healthy

* Heal for free at the **Pokémon Center** (`/pokecenter`).
* Craft or buy a **Healing Machine** for your base. The recipe is 3 Copper Ingots, 4 Iron Ingots, 1 Redstone Dust and 1 Max Revive.
* Some ranks get `/pokeheal`, which heals your whole party anywhere.

***

## 📖 4. What to do next

* ⚔️ Take on the gyms with `/gyms`. Beat 4 gyms in one region to unlock the End.
* 📜 Pick up a daily quest with `/quest`.
* 🗳️ Vote with `/vote` for free crate keys every day.
* 📕 Claim Pokédex rewards as you catch more species with `/dexrewards`.
