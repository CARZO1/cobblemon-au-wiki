# 🦣 Alphas

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Alphas are giant versions of wild Pokémon. They're bigger, tougher and rarer than normal, and they drop much better loot when you knock them out.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🔍 Finding Alphas

* **In the wild.** Alphas spawn in herds, in the same biomes as the normal version. They're rare, so keep an eye out for an oversized Pokémon in the distance.
* **Fossils.** Every revived fossil has a 1 in 800 chance to come out as an Alpha.
* **Honey.** Pokémon drawn in by Honey have a 1 in 4,000 chance to be an Alpha.
* **Hopo Berry bait.** Fishing with Hopo Berries gives a small chance of reeling in an Alpha.

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ Alphas hit hard. Bring a strong team before you start a fight with one.
{% endhint %}

***

## 💎 Alpha drops

Knock out a wild Alpha and you can get:

| Drop | Chance |
| --- | --- |
| 💎 **A random Mega Stone** | 1 in 5. It can be any of 92 Mega Stones, including Mewtwonite X and Y, Latiasite, Latiosite, Darkranite, Heatranite and Zygardite. |
| 🍬 **EXP Candies** | Higher level Alphas drop bigger candies, and the strongest can drop Rare Candies |
| 💪 **Stat candies** | From higher level Alphas |
| 💠 **Type Gems** | Matching the Alpha's type |

***

## 🃏 Alpha Card & Mini Card

These cards change the size of a Pokémon you already own. Send out your Pokémon, then right-click it while holding the card. The card is only used up if it works.

| Card | What it does | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔴 **Alpha Card** | Turns your Pokémon into an Alpha | Glory Shop, Battle Tower BP Shop, Team Rocket Crate |
| 💨 **Mini Card** | Shrinks your Pokémon to half size | Glory Shop, Team Rocket Crate |

* Neither card works on a Pokémon that's already an Alpha.
* Clearing the Nuzlocke Challenge gives a small chance of an Alpha Card too.

***

## 🎯 Alpha bounties

The [PokéHunt](pokehunt.md) board has a weekly **Alpha** page with three Alpha targets. Catch one before anyone else for a big reward.
