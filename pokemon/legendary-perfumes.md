# 🌸 Legendary & Mythical Perfumes

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Spray a <strong>Legendary Perfume</strong> or <strong>Mythical Perfume</strong> and, for the next 30 minutes, you have a much better chance of a Legendary or Mythical finding you. Where you stand matters, so read on before you use one.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🛒 Getting one

Both are sold at the **PokéMart on Survival**. Talk to the perfume seller and click again to confirm.

| Perfume | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 🟡 **Legendary Perfume** | $250,000 |
| 🟣 **Mythical Perfume** | $400,000 |

Right-click it to use it.

***

## 🧭 How it works

1. **You spray it.** For the next **30 minutes**, something Legendary (or Mythical) may catch the scent.
2. **If it does, you'll see:** *"Something mythical has caught the scent..."* Stay where you are, out in the wild.
3. **It comes to you.** Within a few minutes it spawns nearby, and you'll get a message telling you what it is and roughly how far away.

If nothing catches the scent in 30 minutes, the perfume runs out.

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ **A perfume can only bring out a Pokémon that can naturally spawn where you're standing.** Spray it at your base, in town or in the wrong biome and nothing can come. When you use one, you're told which Legendaries or Mythicals could find you there, or that none can.
{% endhint %}

***

## 🗺️ Where to use a Mythical Perfume

Mythicals only live in a few places, so pick your spot carefully:

| Where | Mythicals that can appear |
| --- | --- |
| 🌸 Flower biomes | Mew, Shaymin, Meloetta, Magearna |
| 🌴 Jungle | Mew, Zarude |
| 🌲 Forest at night | Celebi |
| 🐸 Swamp | Keldeo, Pecharunt |
| 🏜️ Desert | Genesect, Zeraora |
| ⛰️ Mountains (open sky) | Jirachi |
| 🔥 Savanna and volcanic biomes | Victini |
| 🌊 River (near water) | Volcanion |
| ⛏️ **Anywhere underground below Y 30** | Diancie |
| 🌑 Deep Dark | Darkrai, Marshadow |
| 🏛️ Ancient Cities and Strongholds | Meltan |
| 🌌 The End | Deoxys, Jirachi, Hoopa (End Cities) |
| 🔥 The Nether | Genesect, Volcanion, Marshadow |

Some have extra conditions, like time of day or open sky. See [Legendary & Mythical spawns](legendary-spawns.md) for every detail.

Legendaries live in far more places, so the **Legendary Perfume** works in most of the world. That page has the full list for those too.

***

## 📌 Good to know

* **One perfume at a time.** You can't use a second until the first one ends. The second one isn't used up if you try.
* **Kept if you die**, and it follows you when you switch servers.
* **Milk doesn't remove it.**
* If you've just switched servers, wait a moment for your data to load before spraying it.
* The perfume doesn't add to other boosters, and other boosters don't change it.
* Type `/legendperfume` to see your active perfume, how long it has left, and whether something has caught the scent.
