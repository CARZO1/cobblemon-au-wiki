# 🦊 Extra Eeveelutions

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">On top of the 8 official Eeveelutions, Cobblemon AU has <strong>11 extra ones</strong>, so Eevee can now evolve into a Pokémon of every type. Some evolve from Eevee, and most can also be found in the wild.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 📋 Evolution methods

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Official (8)" %}
| Pokémon | Type | How to evolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 💧 **Vaporeon** | Water | Use a Water Stone on Eevee |
| ⚡ **Jolteon** | Electric | Use a Thunder Stone on Eevee |
| 🔥 **Flareon** | Fire | Use a Fire Stone on Eevee |
| 🔮 **Espeon** | Psychic | Level up with high friendship during the day |
| 🌙 **Umbreon** | Dark | Level up with high friendship at night |
| 🍃 **Leafeon** | Grass | Use a Leaf Stone on Eevee |
| ❄️ **Glaceon** | Ice | Use an Ice Stone on Eevee |
| 🎀 **Sylveon** | Fairy | Level up with high friendship while knowing a Fairy-type move |
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Extra (11)" %}
| Pokémon | Type | How to evolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 👻 **Spectreon** | Ghost | Level up in a **Soul Sand Valley** with at least 160 friendship |
| ☠️ **Acideon** | Poison | Reach **level 24** with a **Muk** in your party |
| 🥊 **Champeon** | Fighting | Reach **level 36** while holding a **Focus Band** |
| 🎈 **Aereon** | Flying | Use an **Air Balloon** on Eevee |
| ⛏️ **Minereon** | Rock | Level up in **Dripstone Caves** while holding an **Iron Pickaxe** |
| 🐝 **Hiveon** | Bug | Level up while holding a **Beehive** with a **Beedrill** in your party |
| ⚙️ **Automeon** | Steel | Give Eevee a **Lever** to hold, then use a **Redstone Block** on it |
| 🐉 **Wyveon** | Dragon | Level up in **The End** with at least 160 friendship |
| 🏮 **Dungeon** | Ground | Level up in the **Deep Dark** while holding a **Lantern** |
| ⭐ **Eeveeon** | Normal | Level up with **max friendship** (255) while knowing **Last Resort** |
| 🌈 **Evereon** | Normal | Defeat one of **every other Eeveelution** (all 18) with Eevee, then level up |
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}

{% hint style="info" %}
💡 "Level up" means Eevee has to gain a level while the conditions are met. A Rare Candy or EXP Candy works fine.
{% endhint %}

## 🌍 Finding them in the wild

Every extra Eeveelution except Evereon can also spawn in the wild. They're **ultra-rare**, level 18 to 40, and need to be able to see the sky unless noted.

| Pokémon | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| Spectreon | Magical biomes in total darkness. Also a rare spawn in Soul Sand Valleys (level 20 to 68). |
| Acideon | Swamps |
| Champeon | Windswept Savannas |
| Aereon | Meadows and Windswept Hills |
| Minereon | Anywhere in the overworld |
| Hiveon | Anywhere in the overworld, near a beehive or bee nest |
| Automeon | Anywhere in the overworld, near an iron block and a redstone torch |
| Wyveon | Mountain peaks, but not snowy ones |
| Dungeon | Badlands. Also a rare spawn underground in Abyss biomes. |
| Eeveeon | Anywhere in the overworld |
| Evereon | Doesn't spawn. Evolve it from Eevee. |
