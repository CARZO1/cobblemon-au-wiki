# 💎 Mega Stones, Z-Moves & Tera

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Every battle gimmick is on Cobblemon AU: <strong>Mega Evolution</strong> (including the new Legends Z-A Megas), <strong>Z-Moves</strong>, <strong>Terastallization</strong> and <strong>Dynamax</strong>.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🛒 The gear you need

| Gimmick | You need | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 💎 **Mega Evolution** | A Mega Ring (or Bracelet) and the right Mega Stone held by your Pokémon | Ring: Cobblemon Supplies, at the back of the PokéMart |
| 🔷 **Z-Moves** | A Z-Ring and a Z-Crystal held by your Pokémon | Z-Ring: Cobblemon Supplies |
| 💠 **Terastallization** | A Tera Orb | Cobblemon Supplies |
| 🔴 **Dynamax** | A Dynamax Band | Cobblemon Supplies |

You can also craft the Ring, Z-Ring, Tera Orb and Dynamax Band. Press **Ctrl + O** in your inventory to look up the recipes.

***

## 💎 Getting Mega Stones

Mega Stones **can't be crafted**. Here's how to get them:

| Source | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| 🦣 **Alphas** | Every wild Alpha you knock out has a **1 in 5** chance to drop a random Mega Stone. See [Alphas](alphas.md). |
| 🎁 **Mega Stone Crate** | Has every Mega Stone. Buy keys on the [store](https://cobblemon-au.craftingstore.net/category/545120) (the full prize list and odds are there too), or earn them in game. Open them at `/crates` on the Hub. |
| 🔄 **Mega Stone Exchange** | On the Hub. Trade **6 Mega Stones** (any mix) or **6 Raw Mega Stones / Keystones** (any mix) for 1 Mega Stone Crate key. |

**You can earn Mega Stone Crate keys from:**

* voting (100-vote milestone)
* quests (100 quests claimed, plus some weekly quests)
* Pokédex rewards (200 species)
* rank bonuses (Expert and up)
* Tier 6 raids (3% chance per pouch)
* reaching 1500 Elo in Ranked Singles
* the Supporter Crate and the Nuzlocke Challenge clear reward

***

## ⚙️ Server rules for gimmicks

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 💎 **One Mega at a time** | You can only have one Mega Evolved Pokémon at once |
| 🌟 **Mega outside battle** | You can Mega Evolve outside of battle too, just for looks |
| 💠 **Changing Tera Type** | Use **50 Tera Shards** of the new type on your Pokémon |
| 🔴 **Dynamax** | Only works at **Power Spots**, not anywhere |

Tera Shards can drop from wild Pokémon you knock out, and come in raid pouches and the Glory Shop.

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ Some gimmick items are banned in certain battles. For example, gym battles ban **Gengarite**, **Kangaskhanite**, the **Red and Blue Orbs**, the **Rusted Sword and Shield** and **every Z-Crystal**. Type `/bans` in game to see the full lists.
{% endhint %}
