# Legendary & Mythical spawns

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Almost every Legendary and Mythical Pokémon can be found in the wild on Cobblemon AU. They're all <strong>ultra-rare</strong>, so finding one takes patience, the right place and a bit of luck.</p>
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="success" %}
<p align="center">When a Legendary or Mythical spawns, the whole server gets an announcement in chat. Be quick!</p>
{% endhint %}

💡 Want better odds? See [How to boost your chances](legendary-perfumes.md) to learn about Legendary and Mythical Perfumes.

***

## 📖 Reading the tables

| Note | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ☁️ **Open sky** | Has to be able to see the sky directly. It won't spawn under trees, overhangs or in caves. |
| 🌤️ **Outdoors** | Has to be somewhere sky light can reach. This is about the spot, not the time of day. |
| ☀️ **Day only** / 🌙 **Night only** | Only spawns at that time of day |
| 🌑 **Dark only** | Only spawns underground or in the dark |
| 🌧️ **Rain** / 🚫🌧️ **No rain** | Needs rain, or won't spawn while it's raining |
| **Below / above Y** | Height limits. Press **F3** to see your Y level. |

Legendaries spawn on **Survival** (overworld, Nether and End) and in the **Resource World** on the Hub (overworld biomes only).

## 🎣 Fishing only

These four don't spawn on land. You have to fish them up, and your rod needs the **Lure** enchantment.

| Pokémon | Where to fish | Level | Rod needs | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kyogre | Ocean | 70 | Lure III | ☁️ More likely in rain, and even more in a thunderstorm |
| Regice | Frozen Ocean | 70 | Lure III | ☁️ |
| Manaphy | Ocean | 60 | Lure II or higher | |
| Phione | Ocean | 10 | Lure II or higher | |

{% hint style="info" %}
💡 Poké Snacks can't attract Legendaries or Mythicals. Fishing and normal spawns are the only ways to find them.
{% endhint %}
