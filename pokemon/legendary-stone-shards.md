# Legendary Stone Shards

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">A <strong>Legendary Stone Shard</strong> turns your evolution stones into your very own <strong>7 star Legendary raid</strong>. Fill it with 4,000 stones, awaken it, and take on the Legendary with up to 3 friends.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🛒 Getting a shard

* **Buy one** at the **PokéMart in the End** on Survival for **$75,000**. To get to the End you need the first **4 gym badges of any one region**, then type `/end`.
* **Get very lucky:** 6 star raid pouches have a tiny chance to drop one.

***

## 💎 How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Pick your stone

The **first stone you offer locks the shard to that stone for good**, and the stone decides which Legendaries it can summon (see the table below).

💡 Hold the stone you want in your **off hand** the first time you use the shard. Otherwise it picks the first evolution stone it finds in your inventory.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fill it up

Hold the shard and **right-click**. It takes that stone from your inventory, and **stone blocks count as 9 stones**. Keep going until it reaches **4,000 stones**. The shard's description shows how far along you are.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Awaken it

When it's full, **right-click once more**. The shard awakens into a **Raid Crystal** for a random Legendary from your stone's list.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Take on the raid

Place the crystal anywhere you're allowed to build and start the raid. Up to **4 players** can join, and each player brings **1 Pokémon** with **1 life**, just like the [Seven Star Raids](../events/seven-star-raids.md).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ **You only get one attempt.** Once the raid has been fought, the crystal shatters whether you win or lose, so bring your best team.
{% endhint %}

***

## 🐉 Which stone summons what

| Stone | Legendaries |
| --- | --- |
| ⚡ Thunder Stone | Raikou or Zapdos |
| 🔥 Fire Stone | Entei, Moltres or Chi-Yu |
| 💧 Water Stone | Suicune |
| ❄️ Ice Stone | Articuno, Regice or Chien-Pao |
| 🍃 Leaf Stone | Virizion or Wo-Chien |
| ☀️ Sun Stone | Terrakion, Regirock or Ting-Lu |
| ✨ Shiny Stone | Registeel or Cobalion |

Moon, Dusk and Dawn Stones can't be used in a shard for now.

Every Legendary has its own fight. Expect shields, surprise Tera types, weather, stat changes and a time limit, so a varied team helps.

***

## 🎁 Rewards

Beat the Legendary and everyone on the team gets a **7 star Raid Pouch** and a chance to **catch it**. See [Seven Star Raids](../events/seven-star-raids.md#rewards) for what's in the pouch.

***

## ❓ Good to know

* Shards and crystals are normal items, so you can trade them with other players.
* Each shard only holds one kind of stone. If you want a different Legendary, you need another shard.
