# Lost Lore exclusives

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Cobblemon AU runs <strong>Lost Lore</strong>, which adds Pokémon and forms you won't find in normal Cobblemon: ancient horrors, corrupted legends, crystal Pokémon and more.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🌍 Found in the wild

| Pokémon | Type | Where | Rarity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **MT** | Steel | Anywhere in the overworld | Ultra-rare | Counts as a Legendary. More common at night, in rain and especially in thunderstorms. Can spawn as an Alpha. |
| **MT2** | Steel / Electric | Anywhere in the overworld | Ultra-rare | Counts as a Legendary. More common at night. Can spawn as an Alpha. |
| **Black Fog** | Ghost / Dark | Ancient Cities | Ultra-rare | Counts as a Legendary. Appears with a group of Gastly. Can spawn as an Alpha. |
| **Crystal Onix** | Crystal | Dark places in the overworld | Rare | Counts as a Legendary. Evolves into **Crystal Steelix**. |
| **Black Tyranitar** | Rock / Dark | Villages | Alpha only | Counts as a Legendary. Level 100 with 6 perfect IVs. |
| **Snowman Munchlax** | Ice | Snowy biomes | Rare | Evolves into **Snowman Snorlax** (Ice), which can also spawn in Alpha herds |
| **Acerola's Mimikyu** | Ghost / Fairy | Deep Dark | Ultra-rare | |
| **Lana's Eevee** | Normal | Mansions | Rare | |
| **Kecleon** (special colours) | Normal | Jungles and tropical biomes | Uncommon to rare | |

{% hint style="info" %}
💡 MT, MT2, Black Fog, Crystal Onix and Black Tyranitar trigger a Legendary spawn announcement, so you'll know when one appears.
{% endhint %}

***

## ⚗️ Forms made with items

These forms come from special items. Most are crafted. Press **Ctrl + O** in your inventory to look up the recipes.

| Form | Item | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🌑 **Shadow Mewtwo** / **Shadow Lugia** | Shadow Energy | Use it on Mewtwo or Lugia with very high friendship. It's used up, turns them Shadow type with a Shadow Tera Type, and drops their friendship to 0. |
| 🌑 **Shadow Mega Mewtwo X / Y** | Shadow Mewtwonite X / Y | Held by Shadow Mewtwo. Crafted from a normal Mewtwonite and a Shadow Gem. |
| 🛡️ **Armored Mewtwo** | Binding Armor | Use it on Mewtwo. Use it again to take the armour off. |
| 🛡️ **Mega Armored Mewtwo** | Mewtwonite A | Held by Armored Mewtwo. Crafted from a Mewtwonite, Binding Armor and more. |
| ⏳ **Primal Dialga** | Time Gear | Held by Dialga. Crafted from an Adamant Orb and an Amethyst Shard. |
| 🦠 **Virus Groudon** | Corrupted Wish | Use it on Groudon. Crafted from a Red Orb and a Poison Gem. |
| 🌀 **Illusion Rayquaza** | Dream Catcher | Mismagius uses the Dream Catcher to fuse with Rayquaza |
| 💎 **Mega Crystal Steelix** | Crystalline Steelixite | Held by Crystal Steelix |
| 🐚 **Mega Galarian Slowbro** | Slowbrogonite | Held by Galarian Slowbro |
| 🌸 **Eternal Floette** | Not craftable | Only from Tier 6 raids |

## 🧪 Clone starters

Revive these in a Fossil Machine just like a fossil. Each one comes out with at least **4 perfect IVs**.

| Clone | Revive with |
| --- | --- |
| Clone Bulbasaur | Nether Star + Miracle Seed |
| Clone Charmander | Nether Star + Charcoal |
| Clone Squirtle | Nether Star + Mystic Water |

Clone Venusaur, Charizard and Blastoise can Gigantamax.

***
