# The PokéMart

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">The PokéMart on the Hub is where you buy supplies and sell what you've gathered. Type <code>/pokemart</code> to go there from either server, then right-click a shopkeeper to open their shop.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🐾 Pokémon supplies

### 🔵 PokéMart

The everyday basics: Poké Balls, Great Balls, Ultra Balls and Potions. It also buys back EXP Candies, Rare Candies, Relic Coins and some held items.

### ⚪ Ball Emporium

Every kind of Poké Ball, from Premier and Heal Balls to Net, Nest and Dive Balls. You can sell spare Poké Balls back here too.

### 💊 Apothecary

Healing items, including Potions, Full Restores, Revives and status heals.

### 🍓 Berry Gardener

Common battle berries like Oran, Sitrus, Lum and Leppa.

### 🎒 Cobblemon Supplies

At the back of the PokéMart. Sells the Mega Ring, Z-Ring, Dynamax Band and Tera Orb, plus handy blocks like the PC, Healing Machine and Pasture.

### 🔄 Poké Ball Changer

Sells the item that lets you swap the Poké Ball one of your Pokémon is in.

### ✨ Boosting Items

Perfumes that boost Uncommon, Rare or Ultra Rare spawns, and Incense that boosts your shiny odds. Each lasts 30 minutes.

### 🍰 Poké Chef

Sweets and snacks for your Pokémon.

### 🎁 Surprise Shop

Buy a random item and see what you get.

***

## 💪 EV training

### 💪 EV Supplies

Vitamins like Protein, Iron and Carbos, plus EV feathers, to raise your Pokémon's EVs.

### 🫐 EV Berries

Berries that lower EVs, for when you want to reset a spread. It buys back EV berries and Mochi too.

### 🏋️ EV Training Items

The six Power items, which boost the EVs your Pokémon earns from battles.

***

## 💎 Evolution & minerals

### 💎 Jeweler

Evolution stones, plus ores and ingots. The Jeweler also buys ores, gems, diamonds, emeralds and evolution stones from you.

***

## 🧱 Building & farming

### 🪵 Lumber Yard

Every kind of log. It buys logs back as well.

### 🧱 Quarry & Masonry

Stone, bricks, deepslate and other building blocks. It also buys coal, ingots, diamonds and Netherite.

### 🌿 Natural Materials

Dirt, grass, sand, gravel, mud and other natural blocks.

### 🎨 Decorator's Emporium

Glass, stained glass, terracotta and other decorative blocks.

### 🌾 Farm Materials

Seeds, crops, apples and sugar cane to start a farm.

### 💡 Lighting & Utilities

Torches, lanterns, glowstone, sea lanterns and other light sources.

***

{% hint style="success" %}
💡 Shops buy items back for a lot less than they sell them for. For better prices, sell to other players on the [GTS](gts.md) or in your own [Market](market.md) shop.
{% endhint %}
