# Hub and Survival

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Cobblemon AU is two servers joined together. Your Pokémon, PC, inventory, money and progress go with you, so it doesn't matter which one you're on.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🏙️ The Hub

You arrive here every time you join. The Hub holds the lobby, the regions and most of the server's facilities.

| Area | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| 🏥 **The lobby** | Pokémon Center, PokéMart, quests, crates, Gacha Machines, EV Training Tower, the Hall of Fame and the Seven Star Raid building |
| 🗾 **Kanto** | The Kanto and Johto gyms |
| 🌋 **Hoenn** | The Hoenn gyms |
| 🏔️ **Sinnoh** | The Sinnoh gyms |
| 🏆 **Elite Four** | The Elite Four of every region |
| 🌿 **Safari Zone** | Catch Pokémon with Safari Balls |
| 🏪 **Player Market** | Rent a plot and run your own shop |
| ⛏️ **Resource World** | A fresh world for mining and gathering that resets every week. See [The Resource World](resource-world.md). |

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ You can't claim land or set homes on the Hub. Build your base on Survival.
{% endhint %}

***

## 🌲 Survival

Survival is the main world, and it's where you build.

| Area | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| 🏡 **The overworld** | Your base, land claims and homes. Everything you build here is permanent. |
| 🔥 **The Nether** | The full Nether |
| 🌌 **The End** | The End and End Cities. You need 4 gym badges from one region to get in. |
| 🗼 **Battle Tower** | Back-to-back trainer battles, in the End |
| 💎 **Sinnoh Underground** | Treasure and fossils. Get there through a naturally spawning [Hoopa Ring](hoopa-portals.md). |
| 🔁 **Wonder Trade** | `/wt <slot>` only works on Survival |

***

## 🔀 Switching servers

| To go to | Type |
| --- | --- |
| The Hub lobby | `/hub` or `/lobby` |
| Survival spawn | `/survival` or `/smp` |

Most warps take you to the right server for you. For example, typing `/pokecenter` on Survival takes you to the Hub's Pokémon Center. See [Getting around](getting-around.md) for the full list.

***

## 🤝 What's shared

| Shared on both servers | Only on one server |
| --- | --- |
| Your party and PC | Homes and land claims (Survival) |
| Your inventory | Kits and rank bonuses (Hub) |
| Your money and GTS listings | Wonder Trade (Survival) |
| Your quests and gym progress | Nuzlocke Challenge (Hub) |

{% hint style="success" %}
✅ keepInventory is on everywhere, so you won't lose your items when you die.
{% endhint %}
