# ⛏️ The Resource World

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">The Resource World is a fresh, untouched world on the Hub for mining, chopping and gathering. It moves to a brand new area every week, so you never have to dig through someone else's leftovers.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🚪 Getting in

Type `/resource` from either server. You'll be dropped somewhere random in the current area, high in the sky with a temporary elytra so you can glide down.

***

## 📅 Weekly reset

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Saturday midnight AEST** | The Resource World moves to a fresh area. You get a warning 5 minutes and 1 minute before, then anyone still inside is sent to the Hub. |
| **Overnight** | It's closed while the new area is generated |
| **Sunday 12pm AEST** | It opens again, and there's an announcement in chat |

{% hint style="danger" %}
<p align="center"><strong>Don't build anything you want to keep in the Resource World.</strong> Once it moves on, you won't be able to get back to the old area. Build your base on Survival instead.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 📏 The rules

* Each area is **12,000 by 12,000 blocks**. Walk past the edge and you'll be sent back towards the middle.
* You **can't claim land** here.
* Wild Pokémon, Legendaries, Raid Dens and CobbleSafari portals all spawn here just like in the Survival overworld.
* keepInventory is on, so you won't lose your items if you die.

{% hint style="success" %}
💡 Most gathering quests can be done in the Resource World, so it's a good place to tick off your dailies.
{% endhint %}
