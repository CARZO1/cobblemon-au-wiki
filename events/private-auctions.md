# Private Auctions

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Every few weeks we hold a <strong>Private Auction</strong>, where rare and exclusive items go up for bidding. Some of these items can't be found anywhere else on the server.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 📣 When are they?

Private Auctions don't follow a set schedule. They pop up every few weeks, and each one is announced on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/G48XBcSNk4). The announcement tells you:

* 📅 the date and time (AEST)
* 💰 the **minimum balance** you need to get in
* 🎁 what kind of items are up for auction

***

## 🎟️ Getting in

To take part, you need to have at least the **minimum balance** for that auction in your account.

The minimum changes from auction to auction, so check the announcement. It's always a serious amount of money, so start saving early!

***

## 🏷️ How it works

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Turn up on time

Be online and ready when the auction starts. Items go up one at a time.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Bid

Bid with your in-game money. The highest bid when an item closes wins it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pay and collect

Winners pay their bid and receive the item. Make sure you can actually cover what you bid.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ Only bid what you can pay. The normal [server rules](../getting-started/rules.md) apply, and staff decisions on an auction are final.
{% endhint %}
