# 🏗️ Build Competitions

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Build competitions give everyone a theme and a deadline. Build your best entry, and the top builds win prizes and get shown off to the whole server.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 📣 How they work

Build competitions are announced on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/G48XBcSNk4). Each announcement tells you:

* 🎨 the **theme**
* 📅 when building starts and the **deadline** (AEST)
* 📍 **where** to build and how big your build can be
* 🙋 whether you can enter **solo or as a team**
* 🎁 the **prizes**

***

## 🛠️ Entering

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Read the brief

Check the theme, size limit and location before you start, so your build counts.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Build it

Use your own blocks and your own work. Builds copied from somewhere else don't count.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Submit before the deadline

Follow the submission steps in the announcement, which usually means posting your coordinates and a screenshot. Late entries can't be judged.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

***

## 🏅 Judging

Builds are usually judged on things like:

* how well they fit the **theme**
* **creativity** and originality
* **detail** and how polished they look

Staff decisions are final.

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ Don't touch or grief other players' entries. The normal [server rules](../getting-started/rules.md) apply.
{% endhint %}
