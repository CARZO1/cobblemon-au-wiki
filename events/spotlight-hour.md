# Spotlight Hour

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">During a <strong>Spotlight Hour</strong>, one featured Pokémon spawns <strong>10 times more often</strong> and is <strong>5 times more likely to be shiny</strong>. It's the perfect time to hunt for a shiny or fill your Pokédex.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## 🔦 How it works

| | |
| --- | --- |
| ⭐ **Featured Pokémon** | Staff pick one Pokémon for each Spotlight Hour |
| 📈 **Spawns** | It spawns **10 times more often**, in the places it normally spawns |
| ✨ **Shiny odds** | Its shiny odds are **5 times better**. This stacks with your [catch combo](../pokemon/catch-combos.md) shiny bonus |
| ⏱️ **How long** | Usually an hour. A bar at the top of your screen shows the featured Pokémon and the time left |

A message goes out in chat when a Spotlight Hour starts and when it ends.

{% hint style="success" %}
💡 Type `/spotlight` to see the featured Pokémon, how long is left and where it spawns.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ Only normal wild spawns are boosted. Pokémon from herds, fishing and Poké Snacks spawn as usual.
{% endhint %}

***

## 📣 When are they?

Spotlight Hours are announced in game and on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/G48XBcSNk4), so keep an eye out!
