# Ranked & Glory

{% hint style="info" %}
<p align="center">Climb the ranked ladder, finish the month near the top, and earn <strong>Glory</strong> along the way. Glory is a currency you can only get from ranked battling, and you spend it in the <strong>Glory Shop</strong>.</p>
{% endhint %}

***

## ⚔️ Ranked battles

Type `/ranked` to open the Ranked menu and join the queue. You'll be matched against a player of a similar rank. Want to practise first? `/casual` queues you for a battle that doesn't affect your rank.

* You can queue from either server. Ranked battles take place on the **Hub**, so you'll be moved there for the match.
* Your team has to follow the rules of the current season's format. The menu shows what's allowed.
* Tera is allowed in National Dex seasons. You'll need a **Tera Orb** for it.

***

## 🗓️ Seasons

A ranked season lasts **one month**. **Season 1 ends on 31 October.** After that, a new season starts on the 1st of every month.

National Dex is played every second month, with a different format in between:

| Month | Format |
| --- | --- |
| November | National Dex |
| December | Anything Goes |
| January | National Dex |
| February | Gen 7 OU |
| March | National Dex |
| April | Pokémon Champions |
| May | National Dex |
| June | Monotype |
| July | National Dex |
| August | Ubers |

When a season ends:

* Everyone's rank is pulled **halfway back** toward the starting rank, so the top players stay ahead but the gap closes.
* Wins, losses and streaks reset.
* Season rewards go out based on your final leaderboard position.

***

## ✨ Earning Glory

| How | Glory |
| --- | --- |
| Win a ranked match | **3** |
| Lose a ranked match | **1** |
| Finish the season in the top 100 | **100 to 4,000**, depending on your place |
| Play 10, 25, 50 or 100 ranked matches in a season | **20, 50, 100 or 200** bonus Glory |

* Ranked and Random battles pay Glory. Casual battles don't.
* **Your Glory never resets.** It carries over from season to season.
* The match bonus pays your highest milestone only, and it arrives the next time you join after the month ends.

{% hint style="warning" %}
⚠️ To keep it fair, these don't pay any Glory:

* forfeiting, disconnecting or dying during a match
* very short matches
* more than 3 matches against the same player in one day

There's also a daily limit on how much Glory you can earn from matches.
{% endhint %}

***

## 🏅 Season rewards

The top 100 players in each format get rewards when the season ends:

| Place | Glory |
| --- | --- |
| 🥇 #1 | 4,000 |
| 🥈 #2 to #3 | 2,500 |
| 🥉 #4 to #5 | 1,500 |
| #6 to #10 | 1,000 |
| #11 to #25 | 500 |
| #26 to #50 | 250 |
| #51 to #100 | 100 |

Every place also comes with **cash and crate keys**, and the bigger rewards go to the higher places. The **#1** player in each format also gets an exclusive **Champion title**, and the #1 Singles and #1 Doubles players get **The Special Hat**, which you can't get any other way.

Random battle formats pay a little less Glory than the normal formats.

***

## 🛒 The Glory Shop

Type `/gloryshop` to spend your Glory. The shop has:

| Section | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| 🪙 **Pocket Change** | Ability Capsules and Patches, caps, Poké Balls and more |
| 🌿 **Mints** | Every nature mint |
| 🎒 **Held Items** | Competitive held items |
| 🔧 **Utility** | Silver Caps and other training items |
| 🗓️ **Monthly** | Golden Caps, Master Balls, Master Candy and more |
| 🎩 **Hats** | Exclusive hats |
| 🗡️ **Armory** | Pokémon-themed weapons |
| 👑 **Prestige** | **Arceus**, Shiny Cards, Alpha Cards and Mini Cards |
